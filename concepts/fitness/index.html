Bouldering in Gowanus.

Short walls, thick mats, no ropes. New problems every week, and a coach beside you on your first visit.

Book a first visitOpen every day, 7 am to 11 pm

Classes this week

Groups of six, coached on the wall. Shoes and chalk are included in every class.

    The wall

    Four walls up to 4.5 metres, 45 centimetres of mat under every one, and one wall stripped and set again each week.

    • The caveA 45 degree overhang, 4.2 m tallNew problems on Mondays
    • The slabLeans back from you, 4.5 m tallNew problems on Wednesdays
    • The prowTwo faces meeting at an edge, 4.5 m tallNew problems on Fridays
    • The low wallFor kids and first climbs, 2.5 m tallNew problems every month

    Your first visit

    1. Sign the waiver online

      Five minutes on your phone. Under 18, a parent signs with you.

    2. Shoes and chalk at the desk

      Both are included the first time. Wear clothes you can stretch in.

    3. Learn to fall, then climb

      A coach shows you how to land on the mats, then walks you up three easy problems.

    4. Stay as long as you like

      Most people climb for about two hours, with long rests on the mats.
